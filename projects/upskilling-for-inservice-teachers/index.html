Project Overview

Objectives

Uganda has prioritized digitalisation as a pillar of its development strategy. Yet, many teachers face barriers such as limited digital competencies, lack of access to technology, and insufficient training infrastructure.

  • Bridging the gap by empowering teachers with essential digital skills.
  • Building foundational and advanced digital competencies.
  • Promoting innovative, learner-centered teaching.
  • Aligning with national education digitalisation goals.
  • Establishing a sustainable training model with master trainers.
